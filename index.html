Support and resistance
Support is a price where falls have stopped before. Resistance is a price where rises have stopped before. Levels come from past turning points in the 22K history on this page and can fail.
All indicators
Bullish = prices trending up. Bearish = prices trending down. This is rule-based analysis of the 22K price history on this page (moving averages, momentum, RSI, MACD). It does not include global gold prices, the dollar/rupee rate, interest rates or news, which also move gold. Not financial advice, and gold can move either way after you buy.
